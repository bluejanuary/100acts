100 Acts of Kindness to the Environment
100 Acts of Kindness to the Environment is an invitation to take 100 meaningful steps for our planet. Through simple, everyday acts, participants help restore ecosystems, collect valuable environmental data, and tell a new story of stewardship through creativity and community.
Join the Movement
Ready to turn kindness into real-world impact? Follow these four simple steps to become part of the movement — and the global reveal. Every simple act helps!!
Small action, BIG impacts!!
-	Observe, care for, or plant trees and mangroves
-	Protect and care for our wildlife
-	Pick up litter wherever you see it (as small as collecting a few items during a walk or as large as organizing a group cleanup).
1. Register Your Journey
Every movement begins with a single step. You are already doing a good job. Now, let’s see how it stacks up against what others are doing to see the accumulated impact. Your action inspires others, together let’s make a difference. 
Register to join a worldwide community turning small acts of kindness into lasting impact for the planet.
Start by downloading the app. You’ll then create a free account so your actions can be connected to the project.
2. Choose your act of kindness
Every act in this project contributes to real environmental impact. The app (NAME) will allow your everyday actions to become data that scientists can use to protect ecosystems worldwide.
Acts categories: 
a.	Observe, care for, or plant trees and mangroves.
-	Planting a new tree or mangrove, 
-	Caring for existing trees or mangroves
-	Observing and documenting trees in your neighbourhood, school, or natural areas.
-	Watering young trees, checking on growth, identifying species, or monitoring coastal mangroves.
b.	Protect and care for our wildlife
-	Rescuing wildlife
-	Observing, identifying or documenting wildlife
-	Any act to protect or conserve wildlife
c.	Recycling & Waste Reduction
-	Sorting and recycling plastic, glass, or cans
-	Collecting recyclables during cleanups
-	Reducing waste at home, school, or events. 
d.	Litter cleanup
-	Remove trash/garbage from nature or your communities.
Why does your help matter?
-	Planting trees helps absorb carbon, prevent erosion, and create habitat.
-	Recycling keeps materials out of landfills and reduces pollution.
-	Removing trash helps to protect wildlife and keeps plastics out of waterways.

The App
That app will help capture every act in this project that contributes to real environmental impact. The app will allow you to log your everyday acts of kindness, no matter how small. Your journey inspires others. By doing this, you will allow your everyday actions to become data that scientists can use to protect ecosystems worldwide. Here’s how it works.
-	Perform your acts of kindness for the environment and log them in the app.
-	Use the app to take a photo or video of the act of kindness categories A, B and C
-	For category D, take a photo or video and log the type of garbage (plastic bottles, food wrappers, fishing line, cans, etc.), so researchers can better understand pollution patterns.
-	This will automatically tag the location where the act was committed and show it on the map.
-	Users should be able to link to their social media, where they are encouraged to share photos/videos on their socials and to follow and tag @100ActsMovement on Instagram.
-	Use #100ActsMovement and #100ActsOfKindness when posting photos or videos on social media.
-	People can track their own acts of kindness and get a badge after 50 acts. It will be a virtual badge to encourage them to continue. They can also share on their socials.

Together, we make invisible actions visible.
